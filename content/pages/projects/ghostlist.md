## Raw photos in. Structured listings out.

GhostList is an open-source alternative to subscription cross-posting tools. The planned pipeline removes backgrounds, normalizes product images, extracts brand and item details with computer vision, analyzes sold listings, removes statistical outliers, and generates defensible marketplace copy.

I authored the product brief and visual system, designed the pipeline, and built the first image-processing and vision-extraction nodes. The project is an active prototype; marketplace scraping and publishing remain intentionally outside the current build.

**Role:** Product strategy, system architecture, visual identity, Python development, computer-vision workflow

**Stack:** Python, Pillow, rembg, vision-language models
