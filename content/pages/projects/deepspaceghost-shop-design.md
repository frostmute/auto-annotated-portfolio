## Commerce as a design system

DeepspaceGhost has moved through multiple e-commerce platforms while I tested where brand control, low operating cost, inventory, payment processing, shipping, and production automation meet.

I designed and customized the storefronts, created product photography and graphics, evaluated shopping-cart and CMS backends, and integrated fulfillment and payment workflows. The storefront remains an evolving laboratory rather than a one-platform case study.

**Role:** Owner, art direction, web design, front-end customization, product photography, e-commerce operations

**Tools:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Photoshop, e-commerce CMS platforms, payment and fulfillment integrations
