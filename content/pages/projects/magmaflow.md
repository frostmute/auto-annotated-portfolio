## Agents, tasks, and artifacts in one vault

MagmaFlow explores how an Obsidian vault can act as both a human-readable kanban system and a durable control surface for AI agents. The goal is to keep assignments, source context, progress, and produced artifacts in ordinary local files rather than a separate task silo.

The project is currently at concept and architecture stage. It informed later work on Markdown-backed boards, agent routing, and mission-control dashboards; implementation remains deliberately unclaimed until the Obsidian plugin exists.

**Role:** Product concept, system architecture, workflow design

**Planned stack:** TypeScript, Obsidian API, Markdown, local agent runtimes
