## Digital, physical, ethereal

DeepspaceGhost is my outlet for ideas that do not belong to a client brief. The work spans T-shirts, hoodies, stickers, prints, accessories, and pop-up retail, connected by a visual language pulled from graffiti, astronomy, punk and hip-hop, horror, and pop-culture archaeology.

I handle concept development, illustration, typography, garment graphics, mockups, product selection, print-on-demand and screen-print production, merchandising, and the storefront.

**Role:** Owner, creative direction, illustration, typography, product design, merchandising

**Tools:** Illustrator, Photoshop, CorelDRAW, digital asset management, print-on-demand, screen printing
