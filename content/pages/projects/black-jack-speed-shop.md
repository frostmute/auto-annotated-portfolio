## One identity across the counter and the screen

For Black Jack Speed Shop I translated an automotive retail identity into a WordPress and WooCommerce storefront and a companion set of print, signage, and product assets.

The work covered layout, visual hierarchy, responsive e-commerce pages, custom front-end styling, product presentation, shop signage, promotional graphics, and production-ready mockups.

**Role:** Web design, visual design, front-end development, e-commerce design, print production

**Tools:** WordPress, WooCommerce, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, MySQL, Photoshop, Illustrator
