## Small objects, maximum personality

Fiesta medals are San Antonio's annual collision of identity, fundraising, and spectacle. I designed collectible medals for organizations, businesses, and individuals, working across themes that demanded everything from restrained institutional branding to glitter, moving parts, glow paint, lights, bottle openers, and recorded audio.

The job required fast concept development, illustration, type, mechanical thinking, vendor communication, and production management—often inside the compressed calendar of Fiesta season.

**Role:** Product design, illustration, typography, art direction, production management, vendor coordination

**Tools:** CorelDRAW, Illustrator, Photoshop, PDF/EPS/CDR production workflows
