## The city as found material

I photograph the parts of San Antonio that usually disappear into peripheral vision: painted walls, utility hardware, industrial edges, altered vehicles, strange light, discarded objects, and the overlap between designed space and improvised use.

The series moves between documentary street photography and abstract composition. Capture ranges from phones to interchangeable-lens cameras; post-production is restrained when the subject carries itself and aggressive when color and texture are the point.

**Role:** Photographer, editor, colorist, curator

**Tools:** Canon DSLR, Sony cameras, iPhone, Android, Lightroom, DxO PhotoLab, Nik Collection
