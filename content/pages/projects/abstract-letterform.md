## Language pushed past legibility

This body of work treats letters as structures rather than neutral containers. Graffiti, calligraphy, logos, maze forms, modular geometry, and hand-drawn marks become raw material for compositions that move between writing and image.

The practice spans sketches, scanned marks, vector construction, digital painting, repetition, distortion, and print-ready artwork. Pieces sometimes resolve into a readable word and sometimes remain deliberately unstable.

**Role:** Artist, illustrator, typographer, art direction

**Tools:** Pencil and ink, Wacom, Illustrator, Photoshop, CorelDRAW, scanning and image capture
