## Hundreds of stories cast in metal

As Production Manager at Celebrate Excellence I managed the in-house art team and oversaw order production across awards, promotional items, and custom metal products. I also personally designed more than 350 challenge coins for military units, public-safety agencies, companies, organizations, and private clients.

Each coin required translating a client's identity into a manufacturable object: typography, illustration, plating, enamel, relief, edge treatments, moving parts, and vendor-ready specifications all had to survive the trip from screen to metal.

**Role:** Production management, art direction, client translation, illustration, typography, prepress, vendor coordination

**Tools:** CorelDRAW, Illustrator, Photoshop, PDF/EPS/CDR production workflows, digital asset management
