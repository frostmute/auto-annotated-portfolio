## Making specialized products understandable

At Accessible Designs I worked across product design and manufacturing while also building the visual system that brought custom accessibility products to customers.

I photographed the products, handled color correction and retouching, built and maintained the company website, and produced brochures, signage, and trade-show materials. The challenge was practical: explain fit, function, and construction clearly enough that customers and industry partners could understand products made for highly specific mobility needs.

**Role:** Product photography, retouching, web production, visual design, print production, manufacturing support

**Tools:** Canon DSLR, Photoshop, Lightroom, image capture and digital asset management
