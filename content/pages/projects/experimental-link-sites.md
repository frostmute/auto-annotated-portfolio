## A utility page that refuses to look utilitarian

I used Cargo's flexible visual framework to test how far a simple list of links could move away from the generic profile-card pattern. The two versions explored unconventional page composition, animated elements, oversized type, custom CSS and JavaScript, and deliberately strange interaction.

The constraint was useful: keep the page functional and quick to deploy while treating an otherwise disposable piece of social infrastructure as a small web-design playground.

**Role:** Web design, interaction design, visual design, front-end customization

**Tools:** Cargo, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Photoshop
