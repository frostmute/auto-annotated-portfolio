## Identity with consequences

Department badges carry jurisdiction, rank, history, and public trust in a few square inches. At Celebrate Excellence I designed and redesigned official badges for police departments, sheriff's offices, fire marshals, constables, corrections, courts, state patrol, and federal enforcement and regulatory agencies.

The work combined heraldry, typography, iconography, agency history, and strict production tolerances. Every design had to be distinct, legible, manufacturable, and appropriate for sworn identification.

**Role:** Product design, illustration, typography, art direction, production management, prepress

**Tools:** CorelDRAW, Illustrator, Photoshop, PDF/EPS/CDR production workflows, digital asset management
